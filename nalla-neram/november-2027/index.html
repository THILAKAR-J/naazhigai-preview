Nalla Neram November 2027

20262027

The good hours of each day from sunrise to 10 PM, with Rahu Kalam and Yamagandam already removed.

How this is calculated: Nalla Neram here is built from the Gowri Panchangam for Chennai. We take the auspicious Gowri parts (Amirdham, Uthi, Laabam, Dhanam, Sugam) between sunrise and 10 PM, then remove Rahu Kalam and Yamagandam. Printed wall calendars sometimes list their own Nalla Neram, so the times can differ from those.
