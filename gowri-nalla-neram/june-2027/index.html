Gowri Nalla Neram June 2027

20262027

The five good Gowri parts of each day and night: Amirdham, Uthi, Laabam, Dhanam and Sugam.

How this is calculated: Day (sunrise to sunset) and night (sunset to next sunrise) are each divided into eight equal parts. Each weekday has its own Gowri order. Amirdham, Uthi, Laabam, Dhanam and Sugam are good; Rogam, Soram and Visham are avoided.
