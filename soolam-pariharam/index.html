Soolam & Pariharam

Soolam is the direction considered unlucky for starting a journey on a given weekday. If you must travel that way, take the pariharam before leaving.

WeekdaySoolam (Direction)Pariharam
SundayMerku (West)Vellam (Jaggery)
MondayKilakku (East)Thayir (Curd)
TuesdayVadakku (North)Paal (Milk)
WednesdayVadakku (North)Paal (Milk)
ThursdayTherku (South)Thailam (Balm)
FridayMerku (West)Vellam (Jaggery)
SaturdayKilakku (East)Thayir (Curd)

How to use the pariharam

Before leaving in the soolam direction, take a little of the pariharam item: a bite of jaggery, a spoon of curd or milk, or a touch of balm (thailam) on Thursdays. Then start the journey.
